How to Use these Cursors

Step 1

Select your downloaded zip file. (Click on it once)

Step 2

Click on the pink Extract Box

Step 3

Click the Extract All Button

Step 4

Click the Extract Button on the popup.

Step 5

Double click the file named activate

You now have the cursors

Press Ok button
